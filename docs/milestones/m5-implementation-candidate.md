## Critical implementation decisions

- Both human paddles retain owning-local binary64 state, prior displacement and targets. Horizontal reflection happens once after local displacement/projection operands. Online alone integrates recurrent `u`, mirrors its sign before scale/center addition and compares exact integer-twip closed bounds. Literal31-movement/55.15/one-twip edge/corner fixtures pass; logical tolerances never determine a discrete contact. Prior published boxes, strict plane crossing, coefficients and y-wall→x-wall→end order are preserved.
- Simulation is one synchronous30 Hz transaction. Exactly3 lives each, one decrement per miss, atomic zero-life result,90-tick countdown and19-tick hold. Initial serve is random; rematches alternate the previous initial serve. Match/rally/event identities and per-socket control generations prevent obsolete commands/input from mutating new state.
- Visible blur discards pending/predicted input and fences control without pausing or altering deadlines. Return requires sync→runtime-applied state→resume→enabled acknowledgement→new mouse sample. Queued prior-rally targets are disposed. Rematch clears incompatible history but keeps the socket's fence generation. Hidden pages close separately.
- Health requires runtime-processed fresh state/probe/nonce evidence, not network pongs or control acknowledgements. Waiting/end need no increasing tick; ticking phases need fresh advancing snapshots. Both directions expire at5 seconds; congestion has its own2-second clock. Watchdog precedes queues/physics. Ownership is revoked immediately and lingering sockets forced before the additional1-second budget on a responsive process.
- Validation distinguishes server-finalized from client-known results. Full coherent current-match terminal frames can establish knowledge while closing without reopening control. Already-known results survive closure in that browser session; undelivered results remain unknown. No reconnect, persistence or recovery guarantee is introduced.
- Presentation holds the contractual C−1 incoming proxy with the actual predicted/reconciled local draw geometry for one frame before contact correction/overlay. Actual draw rectangles, preceding/incoming PNGs, pre/post corrections, source ticks/times, snapshot age/underflow, local samples, input capture/wire-send/receipt/consumption/actual first-use and raw offset intervals are available to the test harness. These records need independent image and timing review.
