Two ways to play

Mouse only

Solo

Classic

  • Ten levels with an opponent that gets harder
  • Five lives, a score, and a level bonus that ticks down
  • Runs entirely in your browser, no server or account

With a friend

Online · Private 1×1

  • Create a room and send the invite link
  • Three lives each, with rematches
  • Lag compensation, so a defender is not punished for network delay

Servers

São Paulo and Virginia

A room is created on the server nearest to whoever creates it, and the invite link takes your friend to the same one.

No accounts. If someone leaves mid-match, the match ends with no winner and cannot be resumed.

How to play

Four things to know
MoveSteer your paddle, the near cyan one, with the mouse.
ClickPress while the ball is on your paddle to serve.
SweepMove the paddle as it hits. The ball bends the way you were moving.
PauseEsc pauses. The Sound button mutes effects.

About this project

Independent reconstruction

CurveballNext is a from-scratch reimplementation of Curveball, the 2002 Flash game. The original was studied to recover its rules: collisions, curve physics, timing and the difficulty tables. The game logic was then written independently from that specification.

The simulation is deterministic at 30 Hz and was checked frame by frame against the original running in Ruffle. Equivalence with Adobe Flash Player itself is not proven, and the docs say where behavior is uncertain.

Everything is drawn with Canvas and the sound is synthesized with Web Audio. No original artwork, sound or code is used or distributed.
